# HOLDOUT_V2_012

Compare the dissenting preferences reported in the September and December 2024 Statements: who dissented in each, and did each prefer a smaller cut or no cut?

## Réponse proposée à vérifier

September: Michelle Bowman preferred a 25-bp cut rather than 50 bp. December: Beth Hammack preferred maintaining 4.5–4.75 percent rather than cutting. A smaller cut and no cut are different preferences.
